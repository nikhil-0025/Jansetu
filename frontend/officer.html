Indian Government Integration Architecture & Statutory Governance Rules
• Separation of Sensitive PII from Open Data: Citizen document & identity verifications require explicit Electronic Consent Artifacts (DPDP Act, 2023). Public scheme stats (data.gov.in) are queried without PII.
• Authoritative vs Simulated Truth: Only responses from authorized live/sandbox government endpoints with cryptographic seals are marked Authoritative. Mock adapter responses are clearly tagged Simulated (Non-Authoritative).
• Local Identifier Disclaimer: JanSetu Family IDs (e.g. FAM-1001) are system household accounts, not official national identifiers.
• No Automated Rejection: Incomplete API data or potential duplicate flags must never result in automatic disqualification without officer review.
