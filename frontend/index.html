Core Design Distinctions of JanSetu

Family ID vs Citizen ID

Family ID (e.g. FAM-1001) belongs to the shared household unit. Citizen ID (e.g. CIT-1001, CIT-1002) is permanent and stays with the individual across separations.

Persistent Family Relationships

Household membership and biological relationships are maintained as independent data models. Brothers remain brothers in the registry even after living under separate roofs.

Explainable Duplicate Detection

Never blindly rejects! Transparently explains which rule triggered, which dwelling was matched, and suggests actionable guidance for the human verification officer.

JanSetu AI Saathi

Conversational bilingual assistant providing simple English and Hindi guidance on welfare eligibility, documents, and household separation.
